# Taiwan boundaries

Source: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/taiwan-atlas@2021.9.20/towns-10t.json

Publisher: https://github.com/dkaoster/taiwan-atlas

Derived from Ministry of the Interior town boundaries: https://data.gov.tw/dataset/7441

Downloaded 2026-09-25. Decoded TopoJSON into unprojected GeoJSON; rounded coordinates to six decimals. Counties are the source topology’s merged towns. These are bundled reference boundaries, not a guarantee of current administrative changes. No weather values are included. See taiwan-atlas-LICENSE.txt (MIT); original government data: https://data.gov.tw/license .
